**Promise Ledger** helps small B2B sellers keep customer payment promises visible until they are resolved.

<table>
<tr>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

### 📅 Track Promises
Record promised payment dates and keep commitments visible.

</td>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

### ⚠️ Know What’s Next
Surface **due today** and **overdue** promises for faster follow-up.

</td>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

### ✅ Close the Loop
Update promise dates, mark payments paid, and preserve follow-up history.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

### 📄 CSV Workflow
Import/export promise data for practical daily use.

</td>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

### 💬 WhatsApp Drafts
Prepare manual follow-up messages without auto-sending them.

</td>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

### 🎯 Focused UX
The product answers one key question: **who needs a follow-up next?**

</td>
</tr>
</table>

<p align="center"><strong>Promise → Due Date → Follow-up → Update → Paid</strong></p>
